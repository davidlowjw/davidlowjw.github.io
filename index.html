03 Profile

Singapore

David Low is Head of Technology at Vertex Holdings, where he leads the technology and Labs functions: the proprietary investment platform, AI capabilities, corporate IT and cybersecurity. He is also fractional Chief AI Officer at HeyHi.

Before Vertex he was Director of AI & Machine Learning at Singtel, where he led enterprise AI initiatives and scaled machine learning serving millions of customers. His team's PRISM retention system won at the Asian Innovation Excellence Awards 2025, and he led a ten month migration from Cloudera to Azure Databricks covering 60+ data apps, 90+ ML workloads, 160+ dashboards and 1,000+ users.

Earlier, he was Head of Data Science at CardsPal, a Standard Chartered Ventures backed fintech, and Co-founder and Chief Data Scientist of Pand.ai, a conversational AI startup serving financial institutions. He started as a Data Scientist at the Infocomm Development Authority of Singapore, with research collaborations with CMU and MIT.
